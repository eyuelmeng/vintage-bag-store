Where It All Began

Born from a Simple Obsession

Vintage Bag Store was built on a belief that the best bags were made to last. What began as a small personal collection grew from a deep appreciation for craftsmanship, detail, and timeless design.

Over time, that collection started to attract attention. Friends wanted pieces of their own, others asked where to find similar bags, and before long, a small online shop took shape — focused on curating and rehoming distinctive vintage pieces.

Today, every bag in our collection is carefully inspected, cleaned, and selected before it reaches you. We believe in slow fashion, in choosing quality over quantity, and in owning something that truly stands apart.

Our Philosophy

Slow Fashion, Timeless Style

We believe the most sustainable wardrobe is built on pieces that last. Every vintage bag we offer has already proven its durability — and with proper care, it can last for decades more.

We’re not just selling bags. We’re preserving craftsmanship, reducing waste, and connecting people with items that carry real history and character.
