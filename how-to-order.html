Ask Us Anything
Do you accept returns?

We describe every item as accurately as possible. If a bag arrives significantly different from its description, please contact us within 48 hours and we'll work to make it right.

How do I know the bag is authentic vintage?

Every piece is personally sourced and inspected. We include provenance details where available and are always transparent about condition, era, and any repairs.

Can I reserve a bag?

Yes! We can hold a bag for up to 48 hours while you decide. Just reach out via Telegram or the contact form.

Where do you currently deliver?

We focus on local deliveries to ensure quality handling and a smooth experience. Reach out via Telegram or the contact form to confirm availability in your area
